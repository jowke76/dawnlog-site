Dawnlog Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 October 2026
Who we are: Dawnlog is published by Nenad Jović, an independent developer based in Serbia. Contact: jovic76@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Dawnlog has no account, no sign-in and no server of ours. We never receive your diary entries.
  • Your diary is stored on your iPhone, and in your own device backup if you use one. We cannot read either.
  • The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party software development kits.
  • The only personal data we ever process is what you send us yourself when you contact support.

1. What the app stores on your device

The app stores what you enter in the diary: for each night, the times you got into bed, tried to sleep, woke up for good and got out of bed; how long it took you to fall asleep; how often and how long you were awake; how you rated your sleep quality; optional notes; and, with Dawnlog Pro, optional naps, medication and caffeine entries. It also stores your settings (reminder time, widget preference) and your Pro purchase status.

All of this stays in the app's private storage on your iPhone, protected by iOS data protection. We have no access to it.

Sleep information is health-related data. Because it is processed only on your device, by you, for your own personal use, we do not act as a controller of this data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the Serbian Law on Personal Data Protection.

2. Device backups

Like most apps, Dawnlog's data is included in your device backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup), under your own Apple Account and Apple's terms. We keep backups enabled on purpose, so you do not lose your diary when you change phones. We have no access to your backups. You can exclude the app from iCloud Backup in your iCloud backup settings.

3. Widget

The Home Screen widget shows last night's sleep duration and your average over the last seven nights. The app shares with the widget only the date of that night, these two durations and the time until which they are valid, in the app's private shared container. The widget's content is hidden on the Lock Screen. You can switch the widget to show only "Log last night" in Settings → Hide data in widget.

4. Reports and exports

PDF reports and CSV exports are created on your device and handed to the iOS share sheet; where you send them is your choice. The app deletes its temporary export file after sharing. The name you can type into a report header is placed only in that PDF and is not stored by the app. The file name contains only the date range.

5. Purchases

Dawnlog Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled by Apple. Apple processes the payment under its own privacy policy; we never see your payment details. We receive only aggregate sales statistics from App Store Connect. Purchase status is verified on your device.

6. Reminders

The daily reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device. Its text never includes any diary data.

7. What we do receive

  • Support e-mail. If you write to us, we receive your e-mail address and what you write. We use it only to answer you and keep it only as long as the conversation needs. Please do not send your diary unless you want us to see it.
  • App Store statistics. Apple gives us aggregate download, usage and crash statistics only from users who chose to share them with developers in their iOS settings. These do not identify you and contain no diary data.

We do not use analytics, crash-reporting or advertising services of any kind, and the app does not track you across apps or websites.

8. Deleting your data

Settings → Delete All Data removes every entry from the app and the widget data. Deleting the app removes its local data; your device backup may still contain an earlier copy under your Apple Account.

9. Children

Dawnlog is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly process data from them.

10. Your rights

Because your diary never reaches us, we cannot access, export, correct or delete it on your behalf; the controls in the app do that. For the support e-mails we hold, you can ask us at any time to access or delete them at jovic76@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at this address with a new effective date. If a future version of the app sends data anywhere new, the app will tell you before it does.

12. Not a medical device

Dawnlog is a personal diary. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or give health advice. Values are calculated only from what you entered. For interpretation and health decisions, talk to your doctor.
